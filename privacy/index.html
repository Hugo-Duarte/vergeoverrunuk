Publication policy

Privacy, redaction and responsible evidence use.

The campaign publishes only what is reasonably necessary to explain matters of public interest and maintain an accurate case record.

Core policy

Personal information is minimised before publication.

Original evidence is preserved privately. Public copies are selected or edited to remove unnecessary personal information, including contact details, signatures, identifiable children, uninvolved individuals and vehicle registrations where relevant.

Complaint and disclosure documents are summarised unless a redacted extract is necessary to establish a material point. Private legal advice, without-prejudice communications and unpublished settlement discussions are not placed on the public site.

Current website

No analytics or advertising

This package does not include analytics, tracking pixels, advertising scripts or user-submission forms. Standard server or GitHub Pages logs may still be generated by the hosting provider.

Evidence

Originals retained

Original photographs, videos and correspondence are retained privately to preserve provenance and may be shared with regulators, courts, professional advisers or journalists where appropriate.

Corrections

Accuracy matters

Substantive factual corrections will be considered. A correction may be published without removing a fair record that an earlier statement was made.

Third parties

Right to reply

Organisations named in Case 001 are invited to provide records and formal responses. Personal data not needed to explain the response will be removed.
