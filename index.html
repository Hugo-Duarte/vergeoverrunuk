Case 001

Blyth, Northumberland: recurring overrun beside a property entrance.

Photographs show repeated rutting and mud. Doorbell footage shows different vehicle types crossing the frontage. Council records now show earlier complaints, internal referrals and a continuing dispute about responsibility, technical assessment and adoption.

Open the complete case record
Current status

Complaint closed; substantive questions remain.

Taylor Wimpey has issued its final response and says NCC requested that further consideration and works be paused. The resident has asked NCC to confirm the account, identify the process relied upon and allow proportionate proposals to be assessed on their technical merits.

Campaign method

Evidence first. Attribution always. Corrections welcomed.

Documented fact

Records and media

Statements described as documented are supported by correspondence, plans, photographs, video or formal disclosure.

Attributed position

Organisations speak for themselves

Where one organisation describes another’s position, the wording remains clearly attributed until the underlying record is confirmed.

Fair process

Right to reply

NCC, Taylor Wimpey and Persimmon Homes remain invited to provide factual corrections, records or formal responses for fair publication.

The practical objective

Allow proportionate preventative proposals to be prepared and assessed.

The campaign is not asking NCC to admit liability or approve a predetermined design. The immediate request is a workable route through which Taylor Wimpey can submit options and NCC can assess utilities, access, maintenance, safety and adoption requirements transparently.
