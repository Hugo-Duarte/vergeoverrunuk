# Editorial notes — Case 001

## Publication categories

- **Documented**: supported by a record, photograph, video, plan or disclosure.
- **Attributed**: one organisation's account of another organisation's position.
- **Reported**: resident experience or current procedural intention not independently determined.

## Keep off the public site unless later established

- Claims that NCC or a developer lied deliberately.
- Claims that a statutory breach is already proved.
- Claims that an accident or casualty is inevitable.
- Unsupported numerical projections of future overruns or casualties.
- Police incident reference numbers.
- Full address, private contact details, signatures or unredacted disclosure bundles.
- A specific property-value loss unless professionally assessed.
